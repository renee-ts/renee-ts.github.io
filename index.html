About

I am a first year mathematics PhD student at the University of Toronto (also affiliated with the Vector Institute) advised by Vardan Papyan. As of Autumn 2026, I am also a MATS 11.0 Scholar/MATS 11.1 Extension Scholar, supervised by Daniel Kang.

Research

I am interested in AI security, especially in the context of LLM agents.

Right now I'm working on efficiently jailbreaking LLMs with suffix-based attacks, and benchmarking backdoor auditors for agentic models.

Background

I completed an MSc in Mathematics at the University of Toronto, also under the supervision of Vardan Papyan, and before that, I studied Mathematics and Philosophy at the University of Toronto.

Prior to that, I attended the Telluride Association Summer Program in 2018, taught by Blakey Vermeule and William Flesch.
