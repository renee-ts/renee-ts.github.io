About

I am a first year mathematics PhD student at the University of Toronto advised by Vardan Papyan. I am also affiliated with the Vector Institute.

Research

I am broadly interested in studying the mathematical foundations of deep learning, particularly the optimization dynamics and representations learned by neural networks.

Background

I completed an MSc in Mathematics at the University of Toronto, also under the supervision of Vardan Papyan, and before that, I studied Mathematics and Philosophy at the University of Toronto.

Prior to that, I attended the Telluride Association Summer Program in 2018, taught by Blakey Vermeule and William Flesch.
